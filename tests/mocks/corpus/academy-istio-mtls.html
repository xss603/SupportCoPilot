Academy: Istio Mutual TLS Deep Dive

This Platform Academy lab explains how Envoy sidecars negotiate SPIFFE identities, PeerAuthentication policies, and DestinationRule traffic policies across namespaces.

Verifying Envoy mTLS Handshakes

Use istioctl authn tls-check or inspect Envoy proxy clusters via read-only kubectl exec diagnostics to verify whether STRICT mTLS is active between source and destination pods.

kubectl get peerauthentication --all-namespaces
kubectl get destinationrule --all-namespaces
